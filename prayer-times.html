About these times

Times are calculated astronomically from the date, coordinates and method you choose, and agree with well-known calculators to within a minute or two. Sunni methods include Umm al-Qura, Gulf Region, Kuwait, Qatar, Dubai, Egypt, MWL, ISNA and Karachi; Shia times use the Ja’fari (Leva Institute, Qum) or Tehran method, with Maghrib after the eastern redness has passed. Official mosque timetables sometimes add a few minutes of caution; use the adjustment boxes to match yours. At high latitudes in summer, Fajr and Isha use the angle-based rule. The Hijri date uses the Umm al-Qura calendar and can differ by a day from local moon sighting.
