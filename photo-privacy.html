Photo Privacy

Remove hidden location and camera data from photos, and blur or hide faces, car plates and numbers, privately in your browser.

Your files and inputs stay on your device.

No sign-in, no file uploads, and free to use. These tools process data in your browser, with no analytics or advertising scripts. Inputs and generated secrets are not saved; use Clear or close the tab when finished. Copying or downloading is your choice.

Privacy and practical limits

Works with JPG, PNG and WebP photos up to 50 MB (up to 20 photos at a time for removing hidden data). HEIC photos are not supported; share them as JPG first. Removing hidden data keeps the picture exactly as it is, including its orientation. Blur and pixelate hide details from casual viewing; for passwords, ID and card numbers use a solid box. Keep your original if you need it.
