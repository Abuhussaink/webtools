Video Compressor and Trimmer

Shrink videos to fit WhatsApp or email limits, trim the start and end, or remove the sound, privately in your browser with no uploads.

Your files and inputs stay on your device.

No sign-in, no file uploads, and free to use. These tools process data in your browser, with no analytics or advertising scripts. Inputs and generated secrets are not saved; use Clear or close the tab when finished. Copying or downloading is your choice.

Privacy and practical limits

Videos up to 2 GB are processed on your device with the browser's built-in video encoder, so long or 4K videos can take a while on phones. Keep this tab open until it finishes. The result is an MP4 (H.264 video with AAC sound where supported). Fit a file size aims just under the size you choose. Original quality cuts without re-encoding, so the file can be larger than expected; deleting a middle part or adding fades always re-encodes.
