Privacy and practical limits

Up to 75 MB and 300 pages per PDF. Password-protected PDFs are not supported. Target size and custom quality turn each page into a compressed image: text can no longer be selected or searched, and links, bookmarks, form fields and digital signatures are removed. Light clean-up keeps everything but usually saves less. The target is a best effort; very small targets may not be reachable. Keep your original and check the result before sharing.
