Private English Audio & Video Transcription

Transcribe English audio and supported video files privately in your browser. Edit the transcript and download TXT, SRT or VTT subtitles. No file uploads or sign-in.

Your recording stays on your device.

Speech recognition runs locally in your browser. Your recording and transcript are never uploaded or saved by this tool. URL mode downloads directly from the file host, which can see the download request; processing still stays on your device. No account, advertising or analytics scripts.

A small wait for a private transcript.

On first use, this tool downloads a speech-recognition library and model (about 66 MB). This may take a few minutes. The wait lets transcription happen on your device, keeping your recording private. You can optionally remember the model for future visits; recordings and transcripts are never cached.

English only: Arabic transcription is unavailable while we work on recognition quality. Please choose an English recording.

Before you begin

Use a desktop browser for the best results. Processing can take longer than the recording, especially on phones. Keep this tab open. Up to 100 MB and 30 minutes per file. Longer recordings use more memory; a desktop browser is recommended. You can choose a local file or a direct HTTPS media link that allows CORS downloads. YouTube page links are not supported. Supported formats depend on your browser’s audio decoder: try MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4 or WebM. Some video codecs cannot be decoded here; if a file fails, choose an audio-only version.

English transcription only for now. Arabic and other languages are not currently supported. Names, accents and background noise can still cause mistakes. Review the transcript and timestamps before sharing. Silence may produce incorrect text. No speaker identification or certified transcription is provided.
