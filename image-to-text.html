Image to Text (OCR)

Copy text from photos, screenshots and scanned documents in English and Arabic, with text recognition that runs entirely in your browser.

Your files and inputs stay on your device.

No sign-in, no file uploads, and free to use. These tools process data in your browser, with no analytics or advertising scripts. Inputs and generated secrets are not saved; use Clear or close the tab when finished. Copying or downloading is your choice.

Privacy and practical limits

Text recognition runs on your device using the open-source Tesseract engine, served from this website. Your images are never uploaded. Up to 10 images of 30 MB each at a time. Results are best with sharp, straight, well-lit printed text; handwriting and decorative fonts are often misread. Always check names, numbers and amounts before using the text. Recognising Arabic needs clear, printed text.
