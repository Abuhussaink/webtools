Lock or Unlock PDF

Add a password to a PDF with AES-256 encryption, or remove a password you know, privately in your browser.

Your files and passwords stay on your device.

No sign-in, no file uploads, and free to use. The PDF engine (qpdf) is served from this website and runs in your browser. Passwords are never stored or sent anywhere; use Clear or close the tab when finished.

Privacy and practical limits

Up to 75 MB per PDF. Protection uses AES-256, the strongest standard PDF encryption. A forgotten password cannot be recovered, by us or anyone else, so keep it safe. Removing a password requires knowing it; this tool does not crack passwords. Printing and copying restrictions are honoured by most PDF apps but are not a guarantee. Adding or removing a password can invalidate existing digital signatures.
